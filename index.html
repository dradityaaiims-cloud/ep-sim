    Tips

    • Read the His channel: A (septal atrium), H (His spike), V. AH is AV nodal conduction; HV is His–Purkinje conduction.
    • Find the AV nodal ERP: drive train at 600 ms with an S2 at 400, then press D repeatedly until the S2 no longer reaches the His.
    • Slow the speed to ⅕× to watch where each beat goes on the 3D heart.
    • Drag in the right panel to rotate the heart; scroll to zoom.
